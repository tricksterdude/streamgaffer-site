StreamGaffer

Your home-cinema PC, set up right for every film.

StreamGaffer runs on a Windows PC. It switches the display, HDR and audio to match what is playing, and it checks that your receiver and TV agree.

The StreamGaffer iPhone app is its remote and status window: see what is playing, control the receiver, switch audio and subtitles, and use the phone as a remote and touchpad for the PC.

The iPhone app needs StreamGaffer running on your own Windows PC on the same home network. It is in private testing.
